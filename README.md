# The Daily Compass — Saturday 21 November 2026 in one image

An entry for make.fun's [news-image-20261121](https://make.fun/bounty/news-image-20261121)
bounty: sum up one day's world news in a single image.

The image is a broadsheet front page, **The Daily Compass**, drawn
entirely in code. Its lead story is the Russian missiles and jet drone
that came down in Polish fields beside NATO's Exercise Koalicja —
Warsaw calls it a deliberate provocation, and NATO's chief flies in
as Poland demands a stronger air shield — under a drawn map panel
marking the three impact sites (Nowy Lipowiec, Rudnik nad Sanem,
Molodycz) and the exercise ground at Nowa Deba. A markets band
carries Friday's closes (S&P 500 -3.7%, Nasdaq -5%+, Nikkei -5.06%,
gold -4%), four world briefs cover the Kryvyi Rih ballistic strike
(42 injured, a boy of seven and a paramedic dead), Wall Street's
correction as the AI sell-off deepens, the UK–EU deals signed in
Brussels, and the daraxonrasib pancreatic-cancer trial, and a foot
ticker rolls three more headlines from Riyadh, Hormuz and Mexico.

## The program

`main.py` (Python 3, standard library only) copies the shipped
`entry.png` — the artwork rendered when this entry was built — into
`$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR` (or `/output`). It needs no network and no
third-party packages, and two runs produce byte-identical files.
